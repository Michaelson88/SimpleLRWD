Common Questions & Answers

Q: "What is the difference between your custom-coded pages and a template website builder?"

A: Template and drag-and-drop builders are designed to bundle unnecessary, bloated background code that slows down your load times and limits your layout options. Usually putting faster speeds behind a paywall feature. We hand-code our custom websites from scratch. By stripping away that digital bloat, we deliver ultra-lightweight, lightning-fast pages that offer superior SEO performance, zero platform restrictions, and 100% code ownership tailored exactly to your brand.

Q: "Is it easy for me to make changes to my website myself, or will I always need to hire a developer?"

A: It depends on how your website is built. For a fully custom-coded website, making changes requires editing raw code, which is best handled by a professional to ensure nothing breaks. To give you peace of mind, most of our packages include complimentary support updates for the first year, with affordable maintenance options available for subsequent years. If you know you want to update text, blogs, or images frequently on your own, let us know! We can build your site with a CMS (Content Management System), which gives you an easy-to-use dashboard to update your content without touching a single line of code.

Q: "I know absolutely nothing about computers or the internet. Can I still hire a professional developer to build and/or maintain a website?"

A: YES! Not only can we help you, but we want to help you specifically. Why? We want to show you that even without technical experience, it doesn't have to cost thousands of dollars to get your business seen and heard by your local community.
