Common Questions & Answers

Q: "What is the difference between a landing page and a do-it-yourself website builder like WordPress or other drag-and-drop platforms?"

A: Drag-and-drop builders give you a generic template filled with hidden background code that slows your site down and hurts your Google ranking. We hand-code custom landing pages from scratch. This removes the digital 'red tape,' giving you a sleek, lightning-fast design with zero platform restrictions, maximum SEO capability, and 100% ownership of your code.

Q: "I know absolutely nothing about computers or the internet. Can I still hire a professional developer to build and/or maintain a website?"

A: YES! Not only can we help you, but we want to help you specifically. Why? We want to show you that even without technical experience, it doesn't have to cost thousands of dollars to get your business seen and heard by your local community.

Q: "What is the difference between the Expedite Business Website and the Landing Page?"

A: Our Landing Page package includes two fewer pages and focuses on two dominant SEO keywords tailored to your specific industry. We keep the back-end architecture streamlined by omitting advanced features like a sitemap or a Progressive Web App (PWA). Instead, browsers will naturally discover and index these elements over a standard timeframe of roughly 5 to 8 weeks.
By contrast, the Expedite Business package includes a full sitemap and PWA capabilities. We also fast-track the indexing process, ensuring search engines like Google recognize your website for ranking opportunities within 48 hours.
